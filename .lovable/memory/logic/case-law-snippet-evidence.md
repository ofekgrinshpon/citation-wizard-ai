description: When non-trusted search snippets may prove case details, and the source label used
type: constraint
---
- פ"ד is the Supreme Court printed series only; never ask for or accept פ"ד for district/magistrate cases.
- A non-trusted search result may prove parties/date/court only if one snippet has the exact full docket AND both party names near it.
- In such cases the source origin is "אר״ש" — never a database name, never פ"ד.
- Lower courts: magistrate → electronic database/אר״ש only. District filed before 2007 → check פ"מ first, then database. Modern district → database/אר״ש.
